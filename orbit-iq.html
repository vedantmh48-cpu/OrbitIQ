What is Orbit IQ

Satellite intelligence,
simplified.

Ask a question in your own words — "How is vegetation changing near Mumbai?" — and Orbit IQ resolves the location, selects the right satellite analysis, and returns a clear, visual answer.

No GIS experience needed. Maps, charts and reports come directly from your question.

Privacy Policy

Effective 1 March 2026 · Version 1.0

Overview

Orbit IQ is a satellite-intelligence application that lets you explore Earth and satellite data using natural-language questions. This policy covers both this website and the Orbit IQ application.

On this website

  • No accounts, sign-ups, or logins — we collect no personal information through this website.
  • Release downloads will be published here when they are ready; we do not track who downloads them.
  • No advertising or analytics trackers are embedded in this page.

In the application

  • Questions you type are processed in order to perform the requested analysis. Place or region names you enter are used only to locate the area you asked about.
  • Satellite and environmental data are retrieved from public sources such as USGS and Open-Meteo. Where results are simulated or sample-based, they are clearly labeled as demo content.
  • The application requests only permissions appropriate to the platform and needed to function, such as saving exported reports. It does not access contacts, messages, or personal files.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data. There is nothing to sell: the app is built as an open, transparent project.

Changes

If this policy changes as the project evolves, the updated version will be published here.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to orbitIQ2606@gmail.com, or raised directly with the team through the Smart India Hackathon project portal.
